# Billing FAQ — SynthTel Network

> Synthetic data only. All policies, amounts, and rules are illustrative.

---

## When is my bill generated? [BILLING_CYCLE_001]

Bills are generated on your billing cycle end date (varies by account, visible in MyAccount). You are charged on the same day.

---

## Why is my bill higher this month? [BILLING_HIGH_001]

Common reasons for a higher bill:
1. **Roaming charges:** Day Pass ($10/day) or unexpected international use
2. **Overage fees:** Exceeded data cap — $15 per additional GB on Basic and Standard plans
3. **Mid-cycle add-on:** Wearable add-on ($10/mo), tablet add-on ($20/mo) added during cycle and prorated
4. **Third-party charges:** Premium SMS services billed through SynthTel
5. **Plan upgrade mid-cycle:** Prorated charges for both old and new plan

To review your itemized bill: MyAccount > Billing > Current Bill > View Itemized Charges

---

## What are overage fees? [BILLING_OVERAGE_001]

Plans with data caps (Basic 5GB, Standard 20GB, Family 60GB) charge **$15 per additional GB** once the cap is reached.
Unlimited plans (Unlimited Plus, Family Unlimited) do not have overage fees — data is deprioritized, not cut off.

---

## How do I dispute a charge? [BILLING_DISPUTE_001]

1. Log in to MyAccount
2. Go to Billing > Current Bill > Dispute a Charge
3. Select the charge and provide a reason
4. Disputes are reviewed within 5 business days

For charges over $50 or unauthorized roaming charges, contact support directly — a human agent must handle these.

---

## Can I get a refund for roaming charges I didn't authorize? [BILLING_ROAMING_REFUND_001]

Possibly. Unauthorized roaming charges are reviewed case by case.
- If Day Pass activated because roaming was not disabled on device: typically not refunded (user responsibility to disable data roaming)
- If SynthTel provisioned wrong add-on or charged incorrectly: fully refunded
- Disputes must be filed within 60 days of the charge

**Escalation required** — a human billing agent must process roaming refund requests.

---

## What is the third-party charge on my bill? [BILLING_THIRD_PARTY_001]

Some services (ringtones, games, charity donations) can be billed through your SynthTel account via Premium SMS.
To block third-party charges permanently: MyAccount > Settings > Block Third-Party Charges (takes effect immediately).
To dispute an existing third-party charge: use the dispute process above.

---

## Can I change my billing date? [BILLING_DATE_CHANGE_001]

Yes, once per 12-month period. Contact support to request a billing date change.
Note: the first bill after a date change may be higher or lower than normal due to proration.
**Human agent required** for billing date changes.
