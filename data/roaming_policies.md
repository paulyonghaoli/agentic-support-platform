# Roaming Policies — SynthTel Network

> Synthetic data only. All policies, prices, and country lists are illustrative.

## Overview

SynthTel offers two types of international roaming: **Day Pass Roaming** and **Monthly Roaming Add-Ons**.
No roaming is included in any base plan; it must be added explicitly.

---

## Day Pass Roaming

**Cost:** $10/day (automatically activated when you use your phone abroad)
**Included per day:**
- 500 MB data at local speeds (throttled to 128 Kbps after limit)
- Unlimited calls to US numbers
- Unlimited SMS

**Supported regions (Day Pass):**
- Canada, Mexico (automatic; no pre-activation needed)
- Western Europe: UK, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, Netherlands, Switzerland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Portugal, Austria, Belgium, Ireland
- Asia-Pacific: Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Hong Kong
- Latin America: Brazil, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru

**Not supported (Day Pass):** China, India, Russia, Middle East, Africa, most of Southeast Asia

**Note:** Day Pass charges are per calendar day, not per 24-hour period. One-day charge applies even if you only roam for 1 hour in that day.

## Monthly Roaming Add-Ons

### Add-On: International Roaming 3GB — $30/month [ROAMING_MONTHLY_3GB]
- 3 GB data at full LTE speed abroad
- Unlimited international calls
- Unlimited SMS
- Supported in all Day Pass countries
- Best for: trips of 1–2 weeks per month

### Add-On: International Roaming 10GB — $60/month [ROAMING_MONTHLY_10GB]
- 10 GB data at full LTE speed abroad
- Unlimited international calls (including calls to local numbers abroad)
- Unlimited SMS
- Supported in all Day Pass countries + India, Thailand, Philippines, UAE
- Best for: frequent travelers or month-long trips

### Add-On: Japan/Korea Pack — $25/month [ROAMING_JAPAN_KOREA]
- 5 GB data in Japan and South Korea only
- Unlimited calls and SMS in Japan/South Korea
- Valid for 30 days from activation
- Best for: Japan or South Korea trips up to 30 days
- Note: Does not cover other countries. Activate at least 24 hours before departure.

### Add-On: Europe Pack — $35/month [ROAMING_EUROPE_PACK]
- 8 GB data across all supported European countries
- Unlimited calls and SMS in Europe
- Valid for 30 days from activation
- Best for: European trips or multi-country European travel

---

## Activation Rules

1. Roaming add-ons must be **activated before departure** for guaranteed provisioning.
2. Day Pass activates automatically; no action required, but you must have a compatible plan.
3. eSIM devices: confirm eSIM is provisioned for roaming before travel.
4. Smartwatches with standalone cellular **do not** support roaming independently — the paired phone must have an active roaming add-on.
